Delete your Kisan Mitra account

खाता हटाएँ · खाते हटवा

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Kisan Mitra and log in.
  2. Tap the menu (☰) at the top left, then Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately. On the website, the same option is at the app → menu → Settings.

Option 2: without the app

Email [contact email] from any address with the subject "Delete my Kisan Mitra account" and the mobile number you used to log in. We will confirm the request with that number and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your account and mobile number
  • Your profile (name, village, language)
  • All your photo-check results and feedback
  • Chats saved on the phone where you delete the account in the app

What is kept

Only anonymous totals that cannot identify you (for example, the number of photo checks per day). Leaf photos and chat messages are never stored on our servers. See the privacy policy for details.
