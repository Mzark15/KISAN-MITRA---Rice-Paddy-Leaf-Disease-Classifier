Privacy Policy

Kisan Mitra · Effective date: 1 October 2026

Kisan Mitra ("the app") is an AI advisor for paddy (rice) farmers, operated by MRFOUNDERS ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app collects, why, where it is stored, and how you can delete it. It applies to the Kisan Mitra Android app and website.

1. Information we collect

InformationWhy we use itWhere it is kept
Mobile phone numberTo create your account and log you in with a one-time SMS code.Our servers (Amazon Web Services, Mumbai, India)
Name, village and language (optional)To give advice in your language and relevant to your area.Our servers
Leaf photos you take or chooseSent to our server only to check the plant for disease. We do not keep the photo after the check.Not stored on our servers. A copy stays on your phone inside the app.
Photo check results (disease name, confidence, date, your feedback)To show your result, to improve the service, and for anonymous totals on our internal dashboard.Our servers
Chat messages and spoken questionsSent to our server and to an AI language-model provider (see section 3) to write an answer. We do not store the content of your messages on our servers; we only count how many chats happen.Your phone (chat history inside the app)

We do not collect your location, contacts, or any other files on your phone. The app only uses the camera, photos and microphone when you choose to use them.

2. Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera: to take a photo of a leaf.
  • Microphone: to let you speak a question instead of typing. Speech is turned into text by your phone's own speech service (on most Android phones, Google's).
  • Photos: chosen with Android's photo picker; the app sees only the photo you pick.
  • Internet: to reach our server.

3. Who we share information with

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising. We share only what is needed to run the service:

  • Amazon Web Services (India region): hosting, login and SMS delivery of login codes.
  • AI language-model providers (one or more of: SambaNova, Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, Hugging Face): receive the text of your question, recent messages in the same chat, and the latest photo-check result, only to write an answer.
  • Bhashini (Government of India language platform), if enabled: may convert speech to text or text to speech.
  • Authorities, if required by Indian law.

4. How long we keep information

We keep your account and photo-check results until you delete your account. Anonymous totals (for example, "120 photo checks this week") contain no personal information and are kept.

5. Deleting your data

  • In the app: Menu → Settings → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile and photo-check results from our servers and all chats on that phone.
  • Without the app: see how to request deletion.
  • You can delete individual chats, or all chats, on your phone at any time.

6. Security

All traffic between the app and our server is encrypted (HTTPS). Login tokens on your phone are stored in encrypted storage protected by the Android Keystore. Access to our servers is limited to the staff who run the service and is protected by passwords and two-step verification.

7. Children

Kisan Mitra is meant for adult farmers (18 years or older) and is not directed at children.

8. Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask to access, correct or erase your personal data, and to raise a grievance. Contact us at the address below; we will respond within 30 days.
